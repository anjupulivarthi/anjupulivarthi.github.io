I am an academic researcher focusing on **AI-mediated communication**, **source authenticity**, **moving image studies**, and **Indian Eco-Cinema**. My work explores how algorithmic and synthetic cues shape audience perceptions of media truth, as well as environmental narrative frameworks and sustainable practices in visual media.

Research Focus
======

* **AI-Mediated Communication & Source Authenticity:** Psychological processing of synthetic vs. human sources, algorithmic trust, and spectator perception of media authenticity.
* **Moving Image Studies:** Structural visual cues, technological shifts in film and media, and spectator emotion.
* **Indian Eco-Cinema:** Critical environmental narratives, material waste management strategies, and sustainability frameworks within contemporary film production.

Academic & Professional Background
======

* **Academic Training:** Mass Communication & Journalism (Graduate & Undergraduate)
* **Professional Background:** Journalism, literary publishing, and public relations

Publications & Books
